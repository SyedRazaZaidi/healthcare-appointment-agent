# Appointment Booking Policy

## How to book
Patients can book through the virtual appointment assistant, by calling the front desk, or at reception. Standard appointments last 30 minutes. Appointments are offered Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 17:00 and on Saturday mornings from 09:00 to 13:00, depending on each doctor's working days.

## Booking cut-off
Online booking closes 2 hours before the appointment start time. For anything sooner, patients must call the front desk, who will try to fit them in but cannot guarantee a slot.

## Limits per patient
A patient may hold at most 3 upcoming confirmed appointments at the same time. A patient cannot hold two appointments at the same date and time. To book a fourth appointment, an existing one must be cancelled or completed first.

## Reminders
Reminders are sent by SMS or email 48 hours and 24 hours before the appointment. Patients can choose their preferred reminder channel at the front desk or tell the assistant, which will remember the preference.

## Arrival
Please arrive 10 minutes before the appointment time and bring a photo ID. Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late may be asked to rebook.
