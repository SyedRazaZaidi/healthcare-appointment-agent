# Emergency and Urgent Care Guidelines

## This clinic is not an emergency service
The clinic and its virtual assistant do not provide emergency care. For a possible emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for an appointment.

## Examples of emergencies
Examples include chest pain or pressure, severe difficulty breathing, signs of a stroke such as sudden weakness, facial drooping or slurred speech, heavy bleeding that does not stop, loss of consciousness, a severe allergic reaction, and thoughts of harming yourself. If in doubt, treat it as an emergency.

## Urgent but not an emergency
For a same-day problem that is urgent but not life-threatening, call the front desk before 11:00. Staff will try to arrange a same-day visit but cannot guarantee one. Online booking cannot be used for same-day urgent visits.

## What the assistant cannot do
The assistant cannot diagnose conditions, assess symptoms, interpret results, or advise on medicines. For medical questions, the patient should speak to a doctor, and the assistant can help book a suitable appointment.
