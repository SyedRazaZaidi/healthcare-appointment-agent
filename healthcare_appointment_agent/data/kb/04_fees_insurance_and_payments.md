# Fees, Insurance and Payments

## Consultation fees
Standard in-person consultation fees are: General Medicine $60, Pediatrics $65, Dermatology $90, Orthopedics $100, and Cardiology $120. Telehealth consultations cost $10 less than the in-person fee for the same department.

## Insurance
The clinic works with three insurers: Northstar Health, BlueHarbor and Unity Care. Insured patients pay a co-payment of 20% of the consultation fee at reception, and the clinic bills the insurer for the remainder. Patients should bring their insurance card to every visit. Coverage for specific services is decided by the insurer, not by the clinic or the assistant.

## Self-pay patients
Patients without insurance pay the full consultation fee at reception on the day of the visit.

## Payment methods
Reception accepts card and cash. Payment is due at the end of the visit. Itemised receipts are emailed on request.

## Other charges
Late-cancellation fee: $15. No-show fee: $25. Fees for tests, procedures or minor surgery are quoted by the doctor and are not included in the consultation fee.
