# New Patient Registration

## First visit
Every new patient must have their first visit with a General Medicine doctor. Specialist appointments (for example Dermatology or Cardiology) can be booked only after the first General Medicine visit has taken place. The assistant cannot book a specialist for a new patient.

## What to bring
Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medicines you currently take. New patients should arrive 20 minutes early to complete registration at reception.

## Online pre-registration
Patients can save time by completing the online pre-registration form, which asks for contact details and insurance information. Pre-registration does not replace the first visit and does not collect medical history.

## Children
Children under 16 are registered by a parent or legal guardian, who must be present at the first visit. Paediatric patients are seen by the Pediatrics department.

## Existing patients
A patient counts as established once the first General Medicine visit has been completed. Established patients can book Dermatology and Orthopedics directly. Cardiology requires a referral from a General Medicine doctor.
