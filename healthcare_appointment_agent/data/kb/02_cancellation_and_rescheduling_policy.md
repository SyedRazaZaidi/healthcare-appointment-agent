# Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

## Free cancellation window
Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. Cancellation can be done through the assistant, by phone, or at reception.

## Late cancellations
A cancellation made less than 24 hours before the appointment is a late cancellation and incurs a fee of $15. Rescheduling counts as a cancellation for this purpose: moving an appointment less than 24 hours before its start time is treated as a late cancellation.

## No-shows
A patient who does not attend and does not cancel is charged a no-show fee of $25. After 3 no-shows within 12 months, online booking is suspended and the patient must call the front desk to book.

## Fee waivers
The front desk may waive a fee in documented exceptional circumstances such as a bereavement or a hospital admission. Waivers are decided by the practice manager, not by the assistant.

## Cancellations made by the clinic
If the clinic cancels an appointment, for example because a doctor is on leave, no fee applies. The patient is notified by SMS or email and is given priority when rebooking, with the choice of another time with the same doctor or another doctor in the same department.
