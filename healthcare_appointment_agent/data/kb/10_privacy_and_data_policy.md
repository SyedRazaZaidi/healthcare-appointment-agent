# Privacy and Data Policy for the Virtual Assistant

## What the assistant stores
The assistant remembers only non-clinical scheduling information that the patient has told it, such as preferred times of day, preferred doctor, days to avoid, accessibility needs and reminder channel. It never stores diagnoses, symptoms, medicines, test results or the reason for a medical visit in its memory.

## Patient control
Patients can ask the assistant at any time to forget a saved preference, or all of them. Deleting memory does not affect appointment records held by the clinic.

## Identity and access
The assistant works only with the account of the signed-in patient. It cannot show, change or cancel another person's appointments, even at the request of a family member.

## Human oversight
Booking, cancelling and rescheduling are only carried out after the patient explicitly confirms the exact details. Every such action is recorded in an audit log with the confirmation status.

## Clinical records
Medical records are held in the clinic's clinical system and are not accessible to the assistant. Requests for records are handled by the front desk.
