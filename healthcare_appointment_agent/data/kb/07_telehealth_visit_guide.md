# Telehealth Visit Guide

## Who can use telehealth
Telehealth is suitable for follow-up visits, reviewing results already available, and general consultations that do not need a physical examination. It is not available for first visits, physical examinations, or procedures.

## Availability
Telehealth is offered by General Medicine, Pediatrics and Dermatology (follow-ups only). Cardiology and Orthopedics are in-person only.

## How it works
A secure video link is emailed 30 minutes before the appointment. Join about 5 minutes early from a quiet, private place with a stable internet connection. A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone is needed. No software installation is required.

## If the connection fails
If the video cannot connect within 10 minutes, the doctor will phone the number on file. If neither works, the visit is rescheduled without charge.

## Fees
A telehealth consultation costs $10 less than the in-person fee for the same department. The standard cancellation policy also applies to telehealth appointments.
