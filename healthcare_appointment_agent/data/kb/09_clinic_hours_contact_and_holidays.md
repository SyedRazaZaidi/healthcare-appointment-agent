# Clinic Hours, Contact and Holidays

## Opening hours
The clinic is open Monday to Friday from 08:30 to 17:30 and on Saturday from 09:00 to 13:00. It is closed on Sundays and on public holidays. Appointment times run from 09:00 to 17:00 on weekdays, with a lunch break from 12:00 to 14:00, and from 09:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays.

## Front desk
The front desk can be reached by phone on 555-0142 during opening hours or by email at frontdesk@carebridge.example. For urgent same-day requests, call before 11:00.

## Location
CareBridge Family Clinic, 12 Harbor Lane, Springfield (a fictional address used for this project). Free parking is available behind the building.

## Holiday closures
The clinic is closed on 25 December and 1 January. On 24 December and 31 December it closes at 13:00. Holiday appointment availability is published in the assistant two weeks in advance.

## Response times
Emails to the front desk are answered within 2 working days. Complaints are handled by the practice manager.
