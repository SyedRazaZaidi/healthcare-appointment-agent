# Preparing for Your Visit

## Before you come
Arrive 10 minutes early (20 minutes for a first visit). Bring a photo ID, your insurance card, and a list of the medicines you currently take. Bring any recent reports or letters from other doctors that relate to your visit.

## Fasting
Do not fast unless the clinic or your doctor has specifically told you to. If a blood test has been requested, the instructions will state whether fasting is needed and for how long. The assistant cannot decide whether fasting is needed.

## Clothing
Wear loose, comfortable clothing. Dermatology and Orthopedics examinations may require uncovering the affected area, so choose clothing that is easy to adjust.

## Accessibility
The clinic is wheelchair accessible, with a step-free entrance and an accessible restroom. Patients who need an interpreter or other assistance should mention it when booking so it can be arranged, and the assistant can remember this need for future visits.

## Companions
Adults may bring one companion. Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian.
